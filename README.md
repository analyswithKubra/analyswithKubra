**About Me**

 I started my journey as an academic researcher, working on projects that involved gathering and analyzing data to find meaningful insights. I spent a lot of time reviewing articles, cleaning data, and applying statistical methods to answer key questions. My research focused on real-world topics, like exploring the use of mobile health apps and studying the connection between patient satisfaction and hospital reputation.

 During this time, I published articles such as A Study on Health-Oriented Internet Use and Mobile Health Applications and The Mediating Role of Patient Satisfaction in Hospital Reputation and Patient Loyalty. This work sparked my passion for data and its ability to influence decisions.

 As I continued working with data, I realized that the skills I was developing in research could also solve real-world business problems and guide decision-making. My career naturally expanded from academia into the business world, but the core of my work remained the same—using data to drive insights and make informed decisions.

 Now, with over 4 years of experience, I’ve grown into a data analyst who not only loves diving into the details but also enjoys collaborating with teams, solving problems creatively, and communicating insights in ways that everyone can understand. I believe in the power of data to tell a story, and I always strive to present findings in a way that helps teams make confident, informed decisions.

 I’m always learning, constantly curious, and eager for the next challenge. If my journey resonates with you, or if you're interested in how data can make a difference, let’s connect—I’d love to chat!📊📈💡
